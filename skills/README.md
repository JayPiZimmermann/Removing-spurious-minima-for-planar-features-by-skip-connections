# Condensed skills

Each subfolder contains one self-contained `SKILL.md` with the reusable core of its topic.

- `formalization-architecture/` covers Lean source-tree design, public theorem curation, dependency audits, and safe proof refactoring.
- `formalization-build/` covers targeted Lean builds, compile diagnostics, consumer checks, and verified promotion of public declarations.
- `formalization-certificates/` covers reproducible numerical or symbolic certificates, independent checking, and generated Lean artifacts.
- `formalization-conventions/` covers honest Lean definitions and theorem statements, including hypotheses, naming, nonvacuity, and semantic audits.
- `formalization-research/` covers conjecture testing, counterexample searches, proof-route selection, and durable research evidence.
- `math-exposition/` covers clear reporting of mathematical objects, results, evidence, and open work to readers.
